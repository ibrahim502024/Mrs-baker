# Mrs. Baker

A simple recipe costing tool — figure out exactly how much a batch of cookies, cake, or anything else costs to make, based on your own ingredient prices.

**Live app:** https://ibrahim502024.github.io/Mrs-baker/

## What it does

- **Track ingredient prices** — set a price for any amount and unit you buy in (₹150 for 1.5 litres of oil, ₹60 for 1 kg of flour, ₹200 for 25 eggs, etc.)
- **Build recipes** — add ingredients to a recipe with the amount used, and see the total cost calculated automatically
- **Save recipes with photos** — keep a visual list of everything you've made
- **Works offline, no login** — everything is saved right in your phone's browser

## How to use it

1. Open the link above on your phone or computer
2. Go to **Ingredients** and add what you usually buy, with the price and quantity it comes in
3. Create a new recipe, add ingredients and how much of each you're using, and see the total cost per batch
4. Your ingredients and recipes are saved automatically — just close the tab and come back anytime

## A note on your data

This app stores everything locally in your browser (no server, no account). That means:
- It only remembers your recipes on the device and browser you used to add them
- Don't clear your browsing data / site data for this site, or it'll reset
- It won't sync between your phone and a computer — each device keeps its own list
